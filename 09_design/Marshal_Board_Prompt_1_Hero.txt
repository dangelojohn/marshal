STYLE AND TREATMENT:
Authentic industrial operations software. Dense, calm, utilitarian, and legible from across a room. Type is a clean grotesque sans-serif; all identifiers, times and codes are in a monospace face and are precisely column-aligned. Panel corners are square or very slightly rounded — no pill shapes, no soft cards. The only saturated colours anywhere are the warm amber #E0A458 used sparingly for accents and the red-orange #D7503A used exclusively for exceptions. Everything else is neutral grey and near-black.
