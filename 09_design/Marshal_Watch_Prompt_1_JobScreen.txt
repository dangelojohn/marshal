A photorealistic screenshot of an Apple Watch Ultra screen showing "Marshal" — a freight movement app used by equipment operators on an exhibition floor. Show ONLY the screen content, filling the frame edge to edge in the Watch Ultra's rounded-rectangle screen shape. No watch body, no strap, no wrist, no hand, no background. Pixel-sharp, dead-on, no perspective, no reflection, no drop shadow.

Pure black background #000000 — a true OLED black so the display reads at maximum contrast on a bright dock and burns minimal battery on an always-on screen.

The single most important design fact: this shows ONE JOB AND ONLY ONE JOB. There is no list, no scroll indicator, no tab bar, no navigation. An operator's hands are on a forklift's controls; they get one glance and one physical button press.

LAYOUT, top to bottom, generously spaced:

At the very top, small and quiet in mid-grey uppercase monospace, letter-spaced: "MV00042". Beside it on the right, in the same small grey, "09:41".

Below that, dominating the upper half of the screen, the destination in very large bold condensed sans-serif, pure white, filling the width: "BOOTH 412". This is the biggest element by a wide margin — it is the one thing that must be readable in a fraction of a second.

Beneath it, in medium-weight white sans-serif at roughly a third that size: "CRATE-7".

Below that, a thin horizontal hairline rule in dark grey #2A2A2A spanning most of the width.

Under the rule, two short lines in warm amber #E0A458, medium size, left-aligned: "PUMP TRUCK #2" on the first line, and "DUE 09:50" on the second.

Occupying the bottom fifth of the screen, a wide flat control shaped like a rounded rectangle spanning nearly the full screen width, with a solid 3px warm amber border and a fully transparent interior. Inside it, centred, the single uppercase word "ARRIVED" in bold amber. To the immediate left of that word, a small solid amber circle roughly the size of the text — a deliberate visual echo of the physical Action Button on the side of the watch, signalling that this action is triggered by the hardware button rather than by touching the glass.

At the very bottom edge, in tiny dim grey, the words "press action button".

STYLE: purpose-built industrial wearable software. Extremely high contrast, very large type, generous empty black space. No decorative elements whatsoever. The typeface is a clean condensed grotesque for the large text and a monospace for identifiers and times. Only two colours appear on the whole screen: white and one warm amber. Nothing else.

DO NOT RENDER: the watch case, bezel, crown, strap, or any part of the physical device; a wrist, arm, hand or glove; any background or environment; a list of multiple jobs; a scrollbar; a tab bar or navigation dots; a progress ring or activity rings; a map; a photograph; a chart; emoji; cartoon or flat-illustration icons; a colourful app grid; rounded consumer-app cards; gradients of any kind; glassmorphism; drop shadows; a light or white background; the Apple Watch default watch face; or any generic fitness-app aesthetic.
